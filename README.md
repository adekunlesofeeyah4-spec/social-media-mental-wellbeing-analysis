# Social Media Addiction & Mental Well-Being Analysis

Analysis of social media usage, addiction severity, mental well-being, age, gender, and behavioral characteristics using Python and Power BI.

## Project Overview

This project explores the relationship between social media usage, addiction severity, and mental well-being.

The analysis examines differences across age groups and gender groups while investigating behavioral and demographic characteristics associated with lower mental well-being.

## Dataset

The dataset contains approximately **1,500 participants**.

Participant ages range from **15 to 44**, with an average age of **29.37 years**.

Key dataset measures include:

* Daily social media usage
* Mental well-being score
* Addiction level
* Age
* Gender
* Occupation
* Relationship status
* Loneliness score
* FOMO score
* Self-esteem score
* Primary social media platform
* Late-night usage

## Analytical Questions

### Question 1

How does mental well-being score vary across age groups?

* How do occupation and relationship status vary among the age groups with the highest and lowest well-being?

### Question 2

Which primary platform has the highest daily usage hours?

* Are these platforms being used more late at night?

### Question 3

How does mental well-being score relate to addiction level?

* Is well-being consistently decreasing as addiction severity increases?

### Question 4

How does mental well-being score differ across gender groups?

* What characteristics are associated with lower well-being within each gender?

## Analysis

The analysis examined:

* Mental well-being across age groups
* Social media usage by platform
* Late-night usage patterns
* Addiction severity
* Mental well-being by gender
* Occupation and relationship status
* Loneliness, FOMO, and self-esteem

## Key Findings

### Overall Dataset

* Average daily social media usage was **4.52 hours**.
* Average mental well-being score was **6.43**.

### Platform Usage

* **Snapchat** had the highest average daily usage at **4.95 hours**.
* **Instagram** had the lowest average daily usage at **4.44 hours**.
* Instagram had the highest number of late-night users.
* Snapchat had the lowest number of late-night users.
* TikTok had slightly more users who did not use the platform at night than users who did.

### Age and Well-Being

Mental well-being varied across age groups.

* **Early 40s** recorded the highest average well-being score at **6.54**.
* **Early 30s** recorded the lowest average score at **6.36**.
* Among the Early 30s, students who were single had the highest count.
* Among the Early 40s, working professionals who were single had the highest count.

### Addiction and Well-Being

* High addiction was the most common addiction level across male, female, and non-binary participants.
* Mental well-being decreased as addiction severity increased.
* The analysis showed a consistent negative pattern between addiction severity and mental well-being.

### Gender Differences

Average mental well-being scores were:

| Gender     | Average Well-Being Score |
| ---------- | -----------------------: |
| Male       |                     6.47 |
| Female     |                     6.40 |
| Non-binary |                     6.38 |

The differences between gender groups were relatively small.

### Characteristics Associated With Lower Well-Being

The analysis examined:

* Addiction level
* Occupation
* Relationship status
* Loneliness
* FOMO
* Self-esteem

Across gender groups:

* Participants with **high addiction** had the highest proportion of lower-well-being participants among the addiction categories examined.
* The occupation associated with the highest lower-well-being proportion varied by gender.
* **Single participants** had the highest lower-well-being proportion across the gender groups.
* The lower-well-being group showed higher mean and median loneliness, FOMO, and self-esteem scores across the gender groups examined.
